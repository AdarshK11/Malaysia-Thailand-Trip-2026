Important items

Keep booking IDs private

PNRs and booking references should stay in private notes or a password manager, not on the public website.

Recheck terminals

Terminal and gate information can change. Recheck the airline app, airport boards, and web check-in details the day before each flight.

KUL to HKT time zone

Kuala Lumpur is 1 hour ahead of Phuket, so the 12:30 PM to 1:00 PM timing is expected.

Phuket hotel corrected

Current Phuket stay is Novotel Phuket Resort, Patong. Ignore the old Pullman Panwa plan.

Phi Phi tour check

Verify pickup time, exact itinerary, and Maya Bay access before the tour because restrictions can change.

City of Lights tour

Pickup is Bukit Bintang Kiosk / KL HOHO stop next to Sungei Wang Plaza. Be there around 7:45 PM.

Offline essentials

Save flight tickets, hotel confirmations, maps, passports, insurance, and key addresses offline before departure.
